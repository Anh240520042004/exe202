# PRJ301 - Final Exam Preparation 2024
## Previous Exams with Solutions

### Exam 1: Project Management Basics

**Question 1:** Explain the difference between project and operations.

**Answer:** A project is a temporary endeavor undertaken to create a unique product, service, or result. It has a definite beginning and end. Operations are ongoing, repetitive work that continues indefinitely.

---

**Question 2:** What are the five process groups in project management?

**Answer:**
1. Initiating
2. Planning
3. Executing
4. Monitoring and Controlling
5. Closing

---

**Question 3:** Calculate the critical path for the following project:

| Activity | Duration | Predecessors |
|----------|----------|--------------|
| A        | 3 days   | -            |
| B        | 4 days   | A            |
| C        | 2 days   | A            |
| D        | 5 days   | B, C         |

**Answer:** 
- Path A-B-D: 3 + 4 + 5 = 12 days (CRITICAL PATH)
- Path A-C-D: 3 + 2 + 5 = 10 days

### Exam 2: Agile & Scrum

**Question 1:** What are the three pillars of Scrum?

**Answer:**
1. **Transparency** - Everyone shares a common understanding
2. **Inspection** - Frequently check progress toward goals
3. **Adaptation** - Adjust processes when issues are identified

---

**Question 2:** List the Scrum artifacts and events.

**Artifacts:**
- Product Backlog
- Sprint Backlog
- Increment

**Events:**
- Sprint
- Sprint Planning
- Daily Scrum
- Sprint Review
- Sprint Retrospective

---

### Exam 3: Risk Management

**Question:** What are the 4 risk response strategies?

**Answer:**
1. **Avoid** - Eliminate the risk entirely
2. **Transfer** - Shift the risk to a third party
3. **Mitigate** - Reduce the probability or impact
4. **Accept** - Acknowledge and prepare for impact
